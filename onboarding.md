# Onboarding

This guide explains how to set up a new HUNTME partner operation from access to the first booked candidate.

## Step 0: Choose a path

After receiving access, the first action is to choose one campaign direction:

- Models
- Operators

Then read the starter package:
- Campaign Brief
- Quick Start Guide

## What you need on day one

Make sure the following access and tools are available:

- HUNT ME CRM (`huntmecrm.com`)
- Meta Business Manager
- virtual phone number
- WhatsApp BSP account such as SURI, WATI, or Interakt
- active working profiles in Telegram and WhatsApp

## Setup order

The recommended setup sequence is:

### Step 0: Prerequisites
Prepare all accounts and the virtual number.

### Step 1: Setup
Connect the number to the BSP platform, configure CRM access, and create message templates.

This usually takes about 2 days.

### Step 2: Meta Verification
Complete business verification in Meta.

This usually takes 3–14 days.

### Step 3: Full Launch
Train the team, launch traffic, and book the first candidates.

## What to do during Meta Verification

Use the verification period to prepare the operation fully:

- train recruiters on the scripts
- practice objection handling
- set up lead generation campaigns
- prepare Click-to-WhatsApp flows
- prepare job board traffic if needed

## Before live launch

Before sending live traffic, make sure the following are ready:

- BSP message templates are approved
- the qualification funnel has been tested
- recruiters are trained and ready
- CRM integration is working correctly

## Recruiter training tasks

Before a recruiter starts working live, they should:

- read the introduction to the chosen script
- complete 3 practice runs or calls
- study the objection-handling guide
- learn the qualification scenarios

## Before booking the first candidate

Before the first booking, check that:

- qualification criteria are clear and strict
- age requirements are understood:
  - Models: 18–27
  - Operators: 18+
- passport or ID verification is required
- the correct Zoom slot and link are prepared
- candidate requirements are matched correctly:
  - Models: camera and appearance requirements
  - Operators: PC, camera, and technical requirements

## First 24 hours after launch

During the first day after launch:

- monitor response speed for incoming leads
- send booking confirmation within 5 minutes
- track qualification accuracy
- verify that the funnel works without delays

## Success criteria

Onboarding is considered successful when all of the following are true:

- Meta Business Manager, WhatsApp API, and CRM are fully configured and verified
- the recruiter team is trained and follows the qualification checklist strictly
- the first qualified candidate is successfully booked and shows up for the interview
