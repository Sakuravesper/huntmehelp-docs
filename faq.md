# FAQ

## What is HUNTME?

HUNTME is a B2B talent marketplace that connects international streaming studios with qualified streaming models and technical operators in more than 15 countries.

## Who is this documentation for?

This documentation is for recruitment teams, call center directors, and staffing partners.

## What is the difference between the HUNTME partner platform and this documentation site?

The HUNTME platform (`huntmecrm.com`) is the working CRM system for lead tracking, recruiting, and verification.
This documentation site (`huntme.help`) is the knowledge base with guides, scripts, and operating rules.

## What should a new partner start with?

A new partner should choose one of the two paths, Models or Operators, read the START HERE materials, and follow the onboarding roadmap step by step.

## How does the partner workflow work from start to first candidate?

The process usually follows these stages:

1. **Prerequisites** — prepare accounts and access.
2. **Setup** — configure traffic and BSP / WhatsApp API, usually about 2 days.
3. **Meta Verification** — business account verification, usually 3–14 working days.
4. **Full Launch** — launch acquisition channels, qualify leads, and book interviews.

## What are the first 3 documents a new partner should read?

The first documents are:

- `01_campaign_brief`
- `02_quick_start_guide`
- The call scripts for the chosen role:
  - `04_call_scripts_models`
  - `08_call_scripts_operators`

## What is the onboarding roadmap?

The onboarding roadmap is a step-by-step guide that takes a partner from zero to the first booked candidate interview through three key stages:
Setup → Meta Verification → Full Launch.

## What happens after a candidate is booked?

After a candidate is booked:

- a confirmation message is sent within 5 minutes via Telegram or WhatsApp,
- a reminder is sent 1 hour before the interview,
- a Zoom link is sent 5 minutes before the interview,
- the candidate must prepare an original passport or ID for age verification.

## What is the difference between Models and Operators?

- **Models** are streaming models who work on camera.
- **Operators** are technical moderators who work behind the scenes, moderating chats and streams.

## What kind of people should be recruited for Models?

Models should be neat-looking, open, charismatic girls who are ready to work on camera.

## What kind of people should be recruited for Operators?

Operators should have a powerful PC or laptop, stable high-speed internet, and enough technical skill to work with OBS Studio or Streamlabs.

## What are the age requirements for each path?

- **Models:** strictly 18 to 27 years old.
- **Operators:** strictly 18+.

## Why are separate scripts used for Models and Operators?

The roles have different responsibilities, audiences, equipment requirements, appearance expectations, and objections.
That is why they need different scripts.

## What is the role of call scripts?

Call scripts define the structure of the voice contact:
- greeting,
- short pitch,
- screening questions,
- booking the interview.

## What is the role of messenger scripts?

Messenger scripts guide the chat conversation in Telegram or WhatsApp, help qualify leads step by step, and support reminders and follow-up.

## What should a partner do when a candidate objects or asks difficult questions?

Use the 3-step A-A-R method:

- **Acknowledge**
- **Reframe**
- **Bridge**

Also follow the **Rule of One**: make one soft attempt to handle the objection, then respect the candidate’s choice.

## What technical requirements are needed for Operators?

Operators need:

- a PC or laptop with Intel Core 10–14 generation or AMD Ryzen,
- a discrete NVIDIA GeForce or AMD Radeon GPU,
- stable high-speed internet.

A webcam is not required.

## What basic requirements are needed for Models?

Models need:

- age 18–27,
- a smartphone or PC with a good camera,
- a prepared, well-lit place for streaming,
- a passport or ID for verification.

## Do partners need their own telephony system?

Not necessarily. Recruiting can be done effectively through automated messenger chats such as WhatsApp Business API or Telegram, or through the partner’s existing call center telephony.

## Do partners need their own CRM or tracking system?

For internal work, any convenient system can be used, such as AmoCRM, Bitrix24, or Google Sheets.
However, result tracking and verification are handled in HUNT ME CRM.

## How many calls should an experienced manager make per day?

With messenger automation, one recruiter can manage about 50–80 parallel conversations.
Without automation, the load is usually about 15–25.

## How many interviews per day is the target?

A well-set-up channel or team usually aims for around 300–400 booked interviews per month, which is roughly 10–15+ per day.

## What show-up rate is expected?

A high show-up rate is achieved by replying within the first 5 minutes after booking and by sending duplicate reminders before the interview.

## What is considered a good booking rate?

A good booking rate means that 100% of booked candidates fully match the basic qualification checklist:
- age,
- video communication,
- technical setup.

## When should a partner escalate a question to HUNTME support?

Escalate when there are:
- edge cases,
- technical issues,
- candidates who do not fit the standard qualification matrix.

## What should a partner never promise to candidates?

A partner must never:
- invent answers on the fly,
- improvise platform rules,
- make false financial or technical promises.

## What should a partner do if a candidate says they are not interested?

Use the Rule of One:
- make one soft reformulation attempt,
- if the candidate says no again, end the conversation politely and do not push further.

## What should a partner do on day one?

On day one, a partner should:
- choose the campaign path, Models or Operators,
- make the first 3 training calls,
- read the intro script,
- study the objection-handling guide.

## What should a partner read first?

The main START HERE package:
- Campaign Brief
- Quick Start Guide
- Call Scripts

## What are the next steps after reading the starter documents?

The next steps are:
- train recruiters on the scripts,
- launch lead generation campaigns,
- agree with HUNTME on final partnership terms and reporting format.
