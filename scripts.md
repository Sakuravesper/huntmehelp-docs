# Scripts

This page provides an overview of the core communication scripts used by HUNTME partners. The full versions are stored on the dedicated pages linked below.

## Script types

- **Intro script** — starter greetings and the initial mini-pitch for different traffic types: Warm, Cold, and Referral.
- **Qualification script** — step-by-step screening based on core criteria such as age, passport or ID availability, and PC/camera requirements.
- **Objection handling** — a guide for handling questions and objections using the 3-step approach: Acknowledge → Reframe → Bridge.
- **Booking confirmation** — interview booking confirmation sent within 5 minutes after scheduling.
- **Follow-up script** — regular reminders, including 1-hour and 5-minute reminders with the Zoom link, plus reactivation messages for silent candidates.
- **No-show recovery** — rescheduling templates for candidates who did not join the interview.
- **Other** — specific templates for working conditions, schedules, payouts, and training.

## Who uses these scripts

These scripts are used by both recruiters and team leads.

- **Recruiters** use them as a daily operating guide for qualification and communication.
- **Team leads** use them for onboarding new hires, QA, and handling complex situations.

## Mandatory scripts

The required scripts are:

- Intro script
- Qualification script
- Booking confirmation

These are necessary for the first screening and for recording a candidate in the system.

The supporting scripts are:

- Objection handling
- Pre-interview follow-ups
- No-show recovery

These are used as needed to improve conversion, show-up rate, and lead recovery.

## Usage rules

- Use calls and chats differently, depending on the channel and candidate stage.
- Send the relevant script at the right moment: immediately, after 1 hour, or after a no-show.
- Follow the approved sequence instead of improvising.
- Update wording only through the responsible owner.

## Tone of voice

Communication should always be:

- short and structured,
- friendly and respectful,
- free from pressure,
- free from unrealistic promises.

Follow the one-touch rule: if a candidate refuses, use one soft reply only, then respect the candidate's choice.

## Approved and forbidden wording

### Approved wording

Use the approved templates and validated phrasing for replies, confirmations, reminders, and objection handling.

### Forbidden wording

Do not improvise, pressure candidates, misrepresent the vacancy, or hide the 18+ context where it applies.

## Escalation rules

Escalate to the team lead when a situation is non-standard or when a candidate does not match the required criteria.

## Ownership

The team lead or campaign manager is responsible for keeping script wording up to date.
